# Kettle Simulator — product vision

## Product promise

Turn an ordinary task—boiling a kettle—into a short systemic comedy where the
player understands every cause and can deliberately create increasingly absurd
consequences.

## Working design principles

These principles are a reviewable starting point, not permanent constraints.

1. **Readable cause and effect.** Every state change needs clear visual, audio,
   or UI feedback.
2. **Simple interaction, surprising outcome.** Keep controls small while letting
   systems combine into unexpected situations.
3. **Short satisfying sessions.** A player should see a meaningful result in a
   few minutes.
4. **Failure should be entertaining.** Burning, spilling, breaking, or flooding
   should create new decisions instead of only ending the session.
5. **Small-team scope.** Prefer reusable systemic mechanics over large volumes
   of bespoke content.
6. **Player-controlled escalation.** The player should understand how to stop,
   continue, or intentionally worsen a situation.

## Current core loop

1. Take the kettle.
2. Fill it with water.
3. Place it on the stove.
4. Turn on heat and observe temperature and water state.
5. React to boiling, evaporation, overheating, burning, overflow, and flooding.

## Product questions to validate

- What gives the player a goal beyond observing the simulation?
- Which consequences are funny enough to encourage another attempt?
- What progress, scoring, challenge, or unlock system fits without turning the
  project into a content-heavy game?
- How much information should UI show versus communicate through the world?
